What should my workbook contain?

Exactly three columns: BITS ID (or Student’s BITS ID), Course, and Total Marks. Marks must be whole numbers from 0 to 100; fractions are rejected without rounding. Keep IDs as text if they start with zero.

Leave out students receiving NC. Duplicate IDs within a course are rejected. A new upload replaces this session’s data and range settings.
