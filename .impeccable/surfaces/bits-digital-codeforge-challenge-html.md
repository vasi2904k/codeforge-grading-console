# Grading console surface brief

Mode: Operate. Extension of the existing purple-and-white console; use the agreed Stage 2 plan. No new visual identity or concept tournament. Users review dense marks data at a desk and may check it on mobile. Keep light surfaces for sustained reading.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Put course context, grade boundaries and their effects in a legible grading workspace. Preserve the familiar product vocabulary.

OWN-WORLD: Existing purple primary actions and pale lavender canvas, white work surfaces, Segoe UI system typography, restrained borders, clear numerical alignment. Add accessible states in green and red.

STORY: Import a workbook, inspect one course, adjust its bands with reversible changes, inspect student results and review the complete export.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact identity and prototype notice, four workflow links, upload/context controls, a course title and student count, then analytics beside a dense range editor. The results and inline export review follow in document order. Mobile stacks these sections.

FORM: Local extension of existing surface; no seed applies. The signature interaction is a boundary edit updating distribution, student grades and export ranges together, with course-scoped undo.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
